## 1. What are they?

Think of percentiles as a ranking system.

![GIF: A line of stick figures sorting themselves by height](/images/blog/percentiles_and_quartiles/sorting-people.gif)

* **Percentile ($P$):** The value below which $P\%$ of the data falls.
* **Quartiles:** Landmarks that split the sorted data into 4 equal chunks.

### Visualizing the Split

![GIF: A cake being cut into 4 equal slices](/images/blog/percentiles_and_quartiles/quartile-split.gif)

1.  **Q1 (25th Percentile):** The cutoff for the bottom 25%.
2.  **Q2 (50th Percentile):** The **Median**. The exact middle.
3.  **Q3 (75th Percentile):** The cutoff for the top 25%.

---

## 2. Why do we care?

Averages (Mean) lie when outliers exist. Percentiles tell the truth.

![GIF: One giant person entering a room and skewing the average height](/images/blog/percentiles_and_quartiles/outlier-skew.gif)

* **The Lie:** "Average income is $100k" (Skewed by one billionaire).
* **The Truth:** "50th Percentile (Median) income is $40k".

---

## 3. How to Find Them (Step-by-Step)

![GIF: Numbers shuffling into order and then being highlighted](/images/blog/percentiles_and_quartiles/calc-steps.gif)

1.  **Sort** your list from smallest to largest.
2.  **Find the middle** number. This is **Q2 (Median)**.
3.  **Split** the list into a lower half and an upper half.
4.  **Find the middle** of the lower half. This is **Q1**.
5.  **Find the middle** of the upper half. This is **Q3**.
